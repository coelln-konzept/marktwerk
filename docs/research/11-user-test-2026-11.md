# Research 11: User test with the product owner's client (November 2026)

*Source: a machine transcript of a hands-on walkthrough of the deployed dev app with Jerome (office
lead and main user). Arda drove the app and Jerome tried it. The transcript is noisy: German/English
mix, misheard names and product words ("Market Vic", "cello board" = Trello board, "canbon" = kanban).
This file paraphrases the business-relevant findings and leaves out the personal small talk. ❓ marks
an unclear reading. Items are tagged with IDs (UT-xx) so tasks, commits and PRs can refer to them.
**Chris's stress-test results are a separate input and are not covered here.***

## 1. Summary
- **Overall verdict: very positive.** Jerome said *"This is exactly how I imagined it"*,
  *"I'm very impressed"* and *"I can work with this"*. He particularly liked the Übersicht with its
  weekend and due-today split, snooze, done and undo, the event timeline (Ablauf), search, contacts with
  several people per organisation, vendor tags, the Genehmigungen list, the Kalender layers and dark
  mode.
- **The biggest change is to task handling on the event page.** Jerome doesn't like the kanban board
  (*"overwhelming"*). He wants a **long checklist where done items stay visible and get crossed out
  or turned green**, but **only on the event page**. Everywhere else, done items should keep
  disappearing.
- **Reduce complexity:** drop **key tasks (Kernaufgaben)** for now, take **tasks out of the
  Kalender**, and Jerome **doesn't need the notification bell**.
- **The Kalender is strategic.** Today Jerome's calendar is his *source of truth*, mainly for whether
  a market (the fee for the space or the use rights) **is already paid**. The longer-term idea is a
  **wall screen** showing a calendar-only view. Bruno could check it himself instead of asking, and
  could mark things as paid with undo, and that's all. The agreed order: **first make the Kalender
  work perfectly, then build the kiosk view, then pitch the screen to Bruno (~€2,500).**
- **Small wins:** change an event's status (Entwurf / geplant / bestätigt) in one click; rename the
  event's "Überblick" so it isn't confused with the global "Übersicht"; make whole contact rows
  clickable; add photo or scan upload in Wissen for paper mail from the city.
